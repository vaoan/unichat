# ui.chats — notes for Claude

The chat column and what surrounds it: `ChatListPane` (+ `ChatRowCell`, `SpaceChannelsView`, `FolderView`), `Rail` (networks and badges), `ChatFilter` / `InboxRows` / `FolderRows` / `SpaceTree` (pure, tested), `Spaces`, `FavoritesRow`, `ChatMenus` (a chat's menu), `FolderDialogs` / `FolderMoves`, `NewChatPane`, `CommandBar`, `AccountAlerts` + `AccountAlertBar` (foot of the list), `Avatar`, `TypingDots`; the panel on the right: `SidePanel`, `InfoPane` (+ `ActionGrid`), `ChatOptions` (+ `SaveChoice`, `ExploreBar`). The lists' redraw rules and the info panel's accessibility are in `ui/CLAUDE.md`.

- `InfoPane` is built again only when what it shows changes (`InfoPane.shown`): the window refreshes it on every message, and rebuilding blanked its photos (a flash). Its "Multimedia" is `media/ProfileMedia` (tabs in equal shares, the icon over its count: five side by side with two-digit counts were cut and uneven, `ProfileMediaTabsUiTest`): photos, videos, audio (voice notes and music, with the window's `AudioPlayback`), links and files in tabs (`ProfileMedia.Contents`, pure, newest first), photos whole in their square (`MediaTile` keeps the ratio), hidden per chat from its options (`ChatPreferences.setMediaHidden`, `Flag.MEDIA_HIDDEN`). From any of them to the message it came in (`ThreadPane.jumpToMessage`): a photo's or video's menu (right click, the menu key; a click views it), a link's or file's own button.
- "Nuevo chat"'s search was exact where the network searches (its bridge wants a whole username or number) and case-sensitive on usernames and numbers. The contacts already loaded that match a part of the name, a username or a number (case, accents and a number's spaces aside: `NewChatPane.matching`) show at once; the bridge's results come after them, once each (`merged`; `NewChatSearchTest`).
- Saving what's sent on and what I send off left the person's own photos out of a chat's folder without a word, and they thought saving was broken (Jecht's chat, 2026-11-17). The chat's options say so while it's that way (`chatOptions.autoSaveMineOff`, a `risk-notice` under the two switches, bound to them) (`ChatOptionsUiTest.whileOnlyWhatOthersSendIsSavedANoteSaysMineIsnt`).
- `RestBar` (a `TaskBar` on top) tells the service when Archivados is open, which wakes what rests out of the sync (`matrix/CLAUDE.md`), and what's opened there (`chatOpened` from `MainView.openChat`, which also tells `setOpenChat`, since the chat shown never rests; `serverOpened` from `ChatListPane.setOnSpaceOpened`): an archived one opened there brings its Discord server back. A hidden window doesn't leave Archivados: unseen, the app is quiet already, and re-waking on every show repeated the whole wake. It shows "Reactivando lo archivado de Discord…" with N of M chats, "Reactivando <server>…", a catch-up that couldn't finish and will try again, then "Al día" for 4 s (`later`, which tests run themselves). It's focusable with its whole text for screen readers, and nothing is cut at 160 % or in high contrast (`RestBarUiTest`, `ArchiveOpenUiTest`).
- The quick filters are one segmented bar (`FilterBar`, 2026-11-24): "All" as text, Unread, Favorites and Low priority as icons named in their tooltip and accessible text (`chatList.chip.*`). The chips' `FlowPane` wrapped "Prioridad baja" to a second line at 160 %; the bar's parts share one `HBox` row and never wrap. A `ToggleGroup` is one Tab stop (its chosen part), the arrows go along it, Space chooses; in high contrast the chosen part is a ring inside its fill, not only a colour (`FilterBarUiTest`, `FilterUiTest`).
- "Load older chats" (`service/OlderChats`, only when the service has it): an item "Load older chats…" in the rail's network menu (`Rail.networkMenu`, read as it opens: only for a network with a `plans` step, disabled and saying "Loading older chats…" while a run is under way; the old purple row under the list was too visible for something used rarely, 2026-11-26) and a ⌘K command (`MainView.olderChatsCommand`: the rail's network, else every bridged one with an account, `ChatListPane.olderChatsNetworks`; listed only with a step and no run, the bar has no disabled entries). ⌘K works from any list (Archived, a folder…) on purpose: the sheet warns first (`OlderChatsMenuUiTest.commandKOutsideTheInboxStillAsksEveryNetworkWithAStep`). Both go through `OlderChatsLauncher`, which opens `OlderChatsSheet`, the warning before every load: Cancel focused and doing nothing, Instagram left out unless ticked on that click, a Discord in its pause not run. Progress is `OlderChatsBar` on top (a `TaskBar`), then each network's answer for 6 s (`later`, which tests run themselves). It first said an end only after seeing the run going, and its coalesced redraw sees only the latest state: a run that went straight to DONE (an account gone before Load) or within one frame showed nothing at all. It says any DONE but the one the service had when the bar was built, once (`OlderChatsBarUiTest.aRunThatOnlySaysDoneStillShowsItsResult`). The demo has it only with `-Dunichat.demoOlderChats` (`service/DemoOlderChats`; snapshot skill) (`OlderChatsSheetUiTest`, `OlderChatsMenuUiTest`, `OlderChatsBarUiTest`).
