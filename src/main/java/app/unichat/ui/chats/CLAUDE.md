# ui.chats — notes for Claude

The chat column and what surrounds it: `ChatListPane` (+ `ChatRowCell`, `SpaceChannelsView`, `FolderView`), `Rail` (networks and badges), `ChatFilter` / `InboxRows` / `FolderRows` / `SpaceTree` (pure, tested), `Spaces`, `FavoritesRow`, `ChatMenus` (a chat's menu), `FolderDialogs` / `FolderMoves`, `NewChatPane`, `CommandBar`, `AccountAlerts` + `AccountAlertBar` (foot of the list), `Avatar`, `TypingDots`; the panel on the right: `SidePanel`, `InfoPane` (+ `ActionGrid`), `ChatOptions` (+ `SaveChoice`, `ExploreBar`). The lists' redraw rules and the info panel's accessibility are in `ui/CLAUDE.md`.

- `InfoPane` is built again only when what it shows changes (`InfoPane.shown`): the window refreshes it on every message, and rebuilding blanked its photos (a flash). Its "Multimedia" is `media/ProfileMedia` (tabs in equal shares, the icon over its count: five side by side with two-digit counts were cut and uneven, `ProfileMediaTabsUiTest`): photos, videos, audio (voice notes and music, with the window's `AudioPlayback`), links and files in tabs (`ProfileMedia.Contents`, pure, newest first), photos whole in their square (`MediaTile` keeps the ratio), hidden per chat from its options (`ChatPreferences.setMediaHidden`, `Flag.MEDIA_HIDDEN`). From any of them to the message it came in (`ThreadPane.jumpToMessage`): a photo's or video's menu (right click, the menu key; a click views it), a link's or file's own button.
- "Nuevo chat"'s search was exact where the network searches (its bridge wants a whole username or number) and case-sensitive on usernames and numbers. The contacts already loaded that match a part of the name, a username or a number (case, accents and a number's spaces aside: `NewChatPane.matching`) show at once; the bridge's results come after them, once each (`merged`; `NewChatSearchTest`).
