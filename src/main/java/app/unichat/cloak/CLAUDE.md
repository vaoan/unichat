# cloak — notes for Claude

Protecting a photo from AI training before sending it.

- Protected photos (clip → "Foto protegida contra IA…", `ui/timeline/CloakSend` + `CloakBar`, `cloak/`): Mist's textural attack written anew (Mist is GPL, Unichat 0BSD: no code of theirs), `resources/app/unichat/cloak/cloak.py`: PGD on Stable Diffusion 1.x's image encoder only (34M parameters; the encoder alone was checked against the original to 6e-5) toward Mist's target pattern, in tiles of at most 256 px (the grid moves half a tile every other step: no seams) at up to 768 px per side, the change then enlarged over the photo at its own size (up to 4096 px, the server's 100 MB upload limit), so memory stays ~1.5 GB whatever the picture (the person's hard limit is 4 GB: the script stops itself past it, `check_memory`). The full Mist (with the diffusion network) needs ~12 GB and didn't fit an 8 GB Mac. `CloakLevel` LIGHT/MEDIUM/STRONG = 10/30/100 steps (~3/8/25 min on an M1), each with its own strength passed to the script (`--eps`, `--smooth-share`, `--colour`): light hardly shows, medium lets smooth areas take 70 % (a faint pattern up close), strong is close to Mist's own (16/255, all areas, colour too: a visible grain). The person chose these from a measured comparison on their own artwork. `cloak/CloakTool` installs on first use into `<data>/tools/cloak/`: pinned uv, which brings Python 3.12 and `requirements.txt` (exact versions with hashes, `--require-hashes --no-build`, regenerate with `uv pip compile --generate-hashes --exclude-newer <2 weeks ago>`), plus the encoder weights (Hugging Face) and `MIST.png` (GitHub), both pinned; bump `CloakTool.VERSION` when any of it changes. `cloak/CloakJob`: one at a time for the app, cancellable; the result goes as a `DOCUMENT` through `UploadQueue`. Only Apple-chip Macs and 64-bit Windows (PyTorch has no Intel Mac build). `CloakToolTest` with `-Dunichat.networkTests=true` installs for real and protects a picture.

## Lessons
- A cancelled job's thread can take a while to stop (a download doesn't end at the interrupt): after cancel, dismiss and a new start, its late reports wrote over the new job's status, since `update`/`finish` only checked `busy()`. Each start gets an id and only its own thread's reports are taken (`CloakJobTest.anOldJobStoppingLateDoesntWriteOverTheNewOne`). The script's silence limit is `Processes.Limit.whileTalking`, not a loop of its own over unsynchronized arrays.
- Protected photos looked awful (Mist's cell pattern in colour all over the skin, a grid of tile edges, 768 px): Mist's random start (±16/255 everywhere) was most of the noise at few steps, every pixel got the same budget, and tiles were done one by one. Now no random start, a budget per pixel from its neighbourhood's texture (`budget`: smooth areas 15 % of it), the change mostly in brightness, the moving grid and the photo's own size: PSNR 28.7 → 35.7 dB on kodim04 for the same 15 % move. Compare any change with a measure (PSNR/SSIM and the encoder's move at 512 px) and a crop at 2×, not by eye on the whole picture.
- Flat artwork (cel shading, the person's own use) was protected half as much as a photo: the texture budget let smooth areas take only 15 % of the change, and such art is mostly smooth areas (8 % move toward the pattern against 15 % on a photo). Measured on the real picture with five settings, the encoder's move and a decode through SD's VAE (what the AI "sees"), and the person chose a strength per level. Judge a protection change on artwork too, not only photos.
